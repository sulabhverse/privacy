What this app does

This app is a private automation tool used only by the owner to publish posts to Facebook Pages that the owner manages: Gold Rate Updates, Gold Rates in Nepal and Astrology Nepal. It posts daily gold and silver rates, international gold prices, news highlights and a daily rashifal.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or process personal information from Facebook users or Page visitors. It does not use Facebook Login for the public and does not read users' profiles, messages or friend lists. It only uses access tokens for the owner's own Pages, which are stored securely and are never shared.

Information we share

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information. Public data used in posts (gold rates, exchange rates, public news headlines) comes from public sources such as FENEGOSIDA and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Data deletion

Because the app does not store any personal data, there is nothing to delete. If you believe the app holds information about you, or you want a post removed, email sulabhinc@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
